Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Gaetano Settembre ("I," "me," or "my") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit my website https://gaetanosettembre.github.io (the "Site").

Information Collection and Use

I collect minimal information through the Site:

Contact Form Information

When you contact me through the contact form, I collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message subject
  • Your message content

This information is processed through Formspree, a third-party service. Please refer to Formspree's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

Analytics Data

I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the Site. This helps me improve the website experience. Google Analytics may collect:

  • Your IP address (anonymized)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on the Site
  • Referring website

This data is used solely for analytical purposes and is not personally identifiable.

Statcounter Data

I also use Statcounter for website analytics. Statcounter collects similar anonymized data about website usage.

Use of Information

The information I collect is used for:

  • Responding to your inquiries through the contact form
  • Improving the Site based on visitor behavior patterns
  • Understanding which content is most valuable to visitors

Data Protection

I implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Contact form submissions are handled through encrypted connections
  • Analytics data is anonymized and aggregated
  • No sensitive personal data is intentionally collected

Third-Party Services

The Site uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Formspree: For contact form processing
  • Google Analytics: For website analytics
  • Statcounter: For website analytics
  • Google Fonts: For website typography

These services have their own privacy policies, and I encourage you to review them.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data I hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to processing of your personal data

To exercise these rights, please contact me through the contact form on the Site.

Data Retention

I retain contact form submissions only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry. Analytics data is retained according to the retention policies of Google Analytics and Statcounter.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. I encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Me

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me through the contact form on the Site or via email at gaetanosettembre@gmail.com.
